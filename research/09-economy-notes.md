## Glance insights
1. GDP per capita peaked at 16,285 int$ in 2012; 2022's 10,341 is about the 2006 level (10,237), down 36%.
2. War shock: GDP per capita went from 8,384 (1974) to 2,442 int$ (1976), down 71%; the 1973 peak (8,397) was regained only in 2003 (8,671).
3. Nominal GDP fell from 54.9bn USD (2018) to 20.1bn (2023), the 2003 level, before 26.0bn in 2024.
4. Inflation: 487% in 1987 and about 100% in 1992, 0.2% by 1999 under the peg; 2020-23 was 85%, 155%, 171%, 221%.
5. Remittances went from 13.3% of GDP (2002) to 33.3% (2023); net debt rose from 46% (1992) to 105% (1998), IMF gross debt 183% (2006) and 172% (2019).
