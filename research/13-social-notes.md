## Social state of Lebanon, by decade
- 1940s: Confessional quota society built on the 1932 census; Palestinian refugees arrive in 1948 (about 110,000, reported).
- 1950s: Relatively open, urbanising, with a strong press; Lebanese University (1951), women's suffrage (1953), and the 1958 sectarian crisis.
- 1960s: Chehab-era state building (NSSF 1963, press law 1962), but the Palestinian presence (1969 Cairo Agreement) and inequality sharpen.
- 1970s: Labour and rural protests (Ghandour 1972, Sidon fishermen 1975) give way to civil war from 13 April 1975.
- 1980s: War society: mass displacement, emigration, militia media, Sabra and Shatila; middle-class erosion (unverified).
- 1990s: Amnesty and reconstruction; private satellite TV; early web; emigrant capital and Gulf work carry households.
- 2000s: Israeli withdrawal (2000), the 2005 Cedar crowds and Syrian exit, the 2006 war (about one million displaced), Nahr al-Bared 2007.
- 2010s: Syrian refugee load (over one million registered by 2014, freeze 2015), civic protest cycles (2011, 2015, 2019), domestic-violence law 2014.
- 2020s: Collapse society: poverty about 44% (2022), brain drain, blast, cholera, school freezes; then 2024 and 2026 wars with over one million displaced each time; first municipal vote in nine years (May 2025).

## Conflicts between sources
- Civil-war displacement and exit: 600,000 to 900,000 (Demographics page) versus about 990,000 (Diaspora page) versus "nearly one million displaced" (war page). Different concepts (displaced versus emigrated); not reconciled.
- Diaspora size: 4 to 14 million (Diaspora page) versus 10 to 15 million (Demographics page), and 15.4 million in one quoted line. Definition of "origin" drives the spread.
- Syrian refugee peak: "over 1 million registered by April 2014" (Syria page) and "about 1.2 million" peak versus "over 1.08 million" (Demographics page). Returns 2025: UNHCR about 379,000 to 400,000 versus government claim of over 500,000.
- Palestinians: 174,422 in the 2017 census versus 489,292 UNRWA-registered (2023) versus about 222,000 living there (UNRWA Feb 2025).
- 2026 ceasefire: use 16/17 April (extended 23 April and 15 May) and a 19 June truce per the coordinator's fact-check. The New Arab/IOM report says a ceasefire began 21 June; Al Jazeera (13 Sept) says attacks continue.
- Post-2019 emigration: the AUB page gives 79,134 in 2021 (4.5 times 2020); the earlier 195,433 (2018-2021), 729,000 (2020-2026) and 220,000 (2025) figures were not found on opened pages (the last is kept as hypothesis).
- 2026 displacement: over one million by mid-March (Time), peak 1.2 million in April, about 500,000 still displaced in July (IOM), about 330,000 by September (Al Jazeera); different dates, not a conflict.
- Women's suffrage: sources say 18 February 1953; some histories cite 1952 legislation with conditions. Wikipedia gives 8 February 1953 (and 1952 for the agreement); the entry uses 1953-02-08.
