# R-additions: services, food security, governance and jobs, attacks (2026-10-05)

Research only, nothing built or published. All files validate with python3. No em dashes. Scripts and raw downloads: `cache/v10-radd/` (`services.py`, `wfp.py`, `wgi.py`, `cpi.py`, `ilo.py`, `uis.py`, `raw/`). Refresh = rerun them (UA `LebanonHub/1.0 (research)`, under 2 requests/s).

## 1. Services per town
- `services/facilities.json` (3,574 points, columns kind, name, name_ar, lat, lon, src, place_id). Kinds: hospital 199, clinic 69, pharmacy 1,029, doctor 34, dentist 30, laboratory 8, health_other 13, public_school 1,398, school 528, kindergarten 19, college 41, university 206.
- Sources: OSM health and education facilities, HOT exports on HDX, snapshot 2026-10-03 (ODbL 1.0, credit OpenStreetMap contributors); CERD public schools list, May 2017 (HDX, CC0, 1,404 rows, 6 without coordinates and left out). Derived table stays ODbL (share-alike).
- `villages/P-services.jsonl`: one row per place_id (4,257, zeros included), attr counts hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, public_schools, other_schools, universities. 1,447 places have at least one.
- Method: polygons reduced to their centroid; each point goes to the NEAREST place in `D4-places.jsonl` by haversine distance, if within 3 km (all 3,574 were; median 0.28 km, max 2.8 km). Beirut and other dense areas split between neighbourhood records, so do not compare a city with a village.
- Rules: OSM node and building twins (same kind, same name, within 50 m) counted once (13 removed). `public_schools` = CERD list (it includes some public kindergartens). `other_schools` = OSM school, kindergarten or college NOT within 150 m of a CERD school and not tagged public or government (246 matched and dropped), so it holds private and UNRWA schools plus any public school missing from the 2017 list. `universities` = OSM university campuses and buildings, not institutions. Clinics = OSM clinic or centre only; doctors and dentists are in facilities.json but not in the six counts.
- Gaps: CERD is 2017; OSM completeness varies by area (pharmacies are well mapped, clinics are not). UNRWA schools have no coordinates in the source (71 rows by camp), not used.

## 2. Food
- `food/ipc.json`: IPC acute food insecurity, 13 analyses Sep 2022 to Mar 2026 (periods through Aug 2026). `national` (13 rows), `groups` (Level 1 totals, 260), `areas` (665, about 48 areas per analysis). Each row: analysis, validity (current or first projection), from, to, group, area, phase {1..5, 3+, all: [people, share]}. CC0, HDX `lebanon-acute-food-insecurity-country-data`. Areas carry no pcode in the source ("Aley - Leb", "Aley - Syr"); the build must map names to districts. Latest national: Mar 2026 first projection (Apr to Aug 2026), 24% (1,241,715 people) in phase 3+.
- `series/food-wfp.json`: 436 series from 32 commodity and unit pairs (the WFP exchange-rate row is left out). Ids `food_<commodity>_<unit>_<lbp|usd>_<national|governorate>`: national monthly median across markets (64: LBP and USD per pair), plus governorate series where a governorate has 60 or more months for that pair (186 pairs, 372 series). Aug 2012 to Aug 2026; most baskets start 2017 or 2021. CC BY-IGO, WFP VAM via HDX.
- USD CAUTION: WFP's `usdprice` uses the official rate (1,507.5, then 15,000 in 2023, 89,500 from 2024). From Sep 2019 to Feb 2024 it differs from the market rate by more than 10% (Hub series fx_lbp_usd_market_monthly), so those USD months are LEFT OUT (54 months); LBP is complete. The set of markets changes (6 in Aug 2012, 28 later), noted on each series.
- Build note: `lib.SERIES_GROUPS` has no `food` prefix yet; add `"food": "food-prices"`.

## 3. World indicators (new files in `world/indicators/`, existing ones untouched)
- `GOV_WGI_{CC,GE,PV,RL,RQ,VA}.EST`: World Bank WGI estimate, about -2.5 to +2.5, 1996 to 2025 (years 1996, 1998, 2000, then yearly from 2002), 205 to 207 countries, no aggregates, 9 territories without ISO3 left out. CC BY 4.0. Lebanon 2025: corruption -1.22, effectiveness -1.10, stability -1.27, rule of law -1.06, regulation -0.85, voice -0.59.
- `TI.CPI`: Corruption Perceptions Index 0-100, 2012 to 2024, 181 countries, Our World in Data grapher CSV (2025 scores not in it yet). Lebanon 2024 = 22. LICENCE FLAG: OWID lists CC BY 4.0, the Transparency International site states CC BY-ND 4.0 for its content; scores are published unchanged. Orchestrator decides.
- `ILO.UNE.YOUTH` (15 to 24), `ILO.UNE.FEM`, `ILO.UNE.MALE` (15+): ILOSTAT modelled estimates, 186 countries, 1991 to 2026 (2025 and 2026 are projections), CC BY 4.0. Lebanon 2023: youth 22.7%, women 14.5%, men 9.7%. Total unemployment already exists (`SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS`, same ILO modelled series), so it was skipped.
- `UIS.GER.1` (primary), `UIS.GER.2T3` (secondary): UNESCO UIS gross enrolment ratio, 208 to 209 countries, 1970 to 2025, CC BY 4.0 as stated in the brief. Lebanon 2024: primary 81.8, secondary 63.8. Tertiary skipped: `SE.TER.ENRR` already holds the same UIS series (Lebanon 2023 = 54.4 both). The scout's "GER.1 is pre-primary" was wrong: GER.1 is primary.

## 4. Attacks
- `attacks/insecurity-insight.jsonl`, 1,001 rows, CC BY-SA 4.0, Insecurity Insight via HDX `aid-security-risk-in-lebanon` (published 2026-09-28): health care 815 (2016 to 2026), education 58 (2021 to 2026), aid workers 58 (2006 to 2026), water 70 (2020 to 2026). Row counts and 2025 to 2026 killed totals match the source's Overview sheet.
- EVERY row has lat and lon null (geo precision "censored" throughout) and only admin1 (no admin2), so a point layer is impossible; the Strike map layer can only shade governorates. 19 health rows have no governorate.
- killed, injured, kidnapped, arrested count only the group each sheet tracks (victim_group: health workers, educators and students, aid workers); water rows have null counts. Health workers killed: 570 in total, 409 of them in 2024. Perpetrator is as reported by the source, not a finding. Event descriptions are blank in the public file.
- Left out by instruction: ACLED, and the food systems (470 rows), protection in danger (66) and explosive weapons sheets of the same dataset.
